How to Get Around Yogyakarta: The Short Answer

Compare the listed tours by their stops and duration. For Borobudur and Prambanan in one day, the Borobudur Climb to the Top and Prambanan tour (from $49) is the simpler pick. If you also want sunrise and Merapi and can handle a long day, the three-site tour (from $99) lists Borobudur, Merapi and Prambanan. For the Gunungkidul caves, look at a Jomblang listing with a fixed early start. The light in the cave and the daily visitor cap both reward a planned slot.

The reason is distance. Borobudur is roughly 40 km northwest of the city in Magelang Regency. Prambanan is about 17 km east, on the Klaten boundary. The Merapi jeep tracks are up on the southern slope north of town, and Jomblang Cave is in Semanu, Gunungkidul, about 1.5-2 hours southeast. These places lie in different directions, and the driving between them is easy to underestimate.

Before booking, ask the operator to confirm the departure time, pickup point and return arrangements. Below I go leg by leg through four listings, say what each listing's text actually covers, and say which kind of traveller each one fits.

On Borobudur itself, the relief panels read clockwise, so walk the terraces in that direction if you want the stories in order.

Sunrise, Merapi and Prambanan: A Very Long Day

Setumbu's gate opens around 04:00, and in the dry season the advice is to arrive before 05:00. There is a short uphill walk from the car park to the viewpoint, so allow time for it. Confirm the departure time and meeting point before booking a sunrise option. Setumbu sits about 4 km west of Borobudur, near 400 m elevation, and on clear days the temple appears as a silhouette above the morning mist.

The listing says all rates include the ticket to climb Borobudur.

Who should take it: travellers with only one free day who want the temple, the volcano and Prambanan in one listed day. Who should skip it: anyone who wants to linger. Choosing between the rates is partly a photography question. On a clear dry-season morning the temple terraces can be the better place to shoot, because the light hits the stupas directly. Setumbu is worth it when you specifically want the misty silhouette with the volcano behind. More on both in my Borobudur sunrise guide.

In the dry season of 2017 I took a retired German couple up Setumbu. At the top, the husband asked me to name the finest temple in Java. Out of habit I started to say Borobudur, then stopped and told him it depends what you came to feel: Prambanan reaches up like a prayer, Borobudur asks you to walk inward. He said he trusted me more for not selling him a winner. That distinction is useful when comparing these tours. A day that covers everything suits some travellers. For others it turns the place they cared about into a quick stop.

Getting to Jomblang and Pindul: Why the Start Time Matters

Gunungkidul is not a place to improvise. Jomblang is a vertical cave you enter by a rope descent of roughly 60 m, with the local crew running the belay. Its underground chamber connects by a passage to the larger Grubug cave, which is where the beam of sunlight falls. That beam, Heaven's Light, appears most reliably on sunny days between about 10:00 and 12:00, and the daily cap in that window is commonly cited at around 25 people. Your transport has to get you to Semanu in time for that window, so the departure time is the detail to pin down.

Beyond the City: Distances, Side Trips and Getting Back

If you are arranging the Prambanan side yourself, an early start pays off. Sewu Temple, a large Buddhist complex, is within walking distance just north of Prambanan inside the same archaeological park, and walking there early helps you beat the crowds at the Shiva temple. Ratu Boko, a hilltop palace and gate complex about 3 km south of Prambanan, is a popular sunset viewpoint over the plain. If you stay for sunset, settle your ride back before you go.

Merapi is a separate direction again, north of the city. The jeep lava tours run in the Kaliurang and Kaliadem area across tracks scoured by the 2010 pyroclastic flows, with common stops at the Kaliadem bunker, Museum Sisa Hartaku and Batu Alien. Timing, Tickets and What to Bring

Book the Borobudur structure ticket online before you travel, rather than planning to buy it at the gate. The daily quota is capped, and the terraces require a local guide and the woven upanat sandals, both listed in the structure ticket's price. The official structure ticket page lists climb hours of 08:30 to 17:00 WIB and asks you to arrive 30 to 45 minutes before your session, so time your ride from the city around that. The 06:30 to 16:30 WIB courtyard-only ticket does not include climbing and is listed for domestic visitors only. Check current hours and ticket rules on the official Borobudur Park site. The official Prambanan Free Borobudur package lists both temples' entrance tickets, but its page does not say whether the Borobudur part includes climbing or how long it stays valid. Before buying, check what your tour already includes and whether the ticket covers your intended climb.

Prambanan opens around 06:30 and closes around 17:00. On Mondays its Brahma, Shiva and Vishnu main temple areas close for maintenance while the courtyard stays open, according to InJourney's Prambanan guidance, dated 29 July 2026. The Ramayana Ballet starts around 19:30, so arrange your ride back before the evening if you plan to attend. Ballet tickets are tiered by seating, from around IDR 125,000 up to IDR 450,000 or more, and are separate from temple entry. Check whether your date uses the open-air or the indoor stage on my Ramayana Ballet page.

For Merapi, sunrise jeep departures leave around 04:30 from the Kaliurang side. Kaliurang sits around 900 m and is noticeably cooler than the city, so bring a layer. Jeeps are priced per jeep, not per person, and seat about 4, so a couple pays the same as four people. Join others or accept the higher cost per person. Operators take cash only. The 2010 lava tracks are black volcanic dust, so wear sandals and clothes you do not love, not white shoes. Access zones change with the alert level set by BPPTKG, the volcano monitoring agency, and reputable operators stay inside the permitted radius. If the volcano is your main reason to come, I compare the jeep options in Merapi jeep tours compared.

For the caves, check the weather the evening before, and pack a change of clothes if you add Pindul. Sun between about 10:00 and 12:00 is what you are booking for.

What to Check Before Booking

  • Pickup point and time The VIP temple-sunrise option lists a 3 am pickup, and the Jomblang listing specifies departure from Yogyakarta between 07.00 and 07.30. Confirm the collection point and the timing for your selected option.
  • What the ticket covers Confirm whether the Borobudur climb ticket is included and, on the sunrise tour, which of the three rate options you are paying for. Setumbu Hill and Borobudur are separate tickets.
  • Your climb slot Ask which timed climb slot the operator holds for you, because the daily quota is capped.
  • Jomblang reservation Check that a Jomblang slot is reserved for the late-morning light, and ask what happens on a cloudy day.
  • Length of the day Compare the listed duration with your energy. A 720-minute day with a sunrise start is very different from a 480-minute one.
  • The ride home Confirm outward and return transport for every leg. If you add the Ramayana Ballet or stay for sunset anywhere beyond the city, settle your return arrangements before you set off.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Borobudur from Yogyakarta?

Borobudur is roughly 40 km northwest of the city, in Magelang Regency. Prambanan is much closer, about 17 km east. Plan them as separate legs and allow for travel between them.

Can I do Borobudur, Merapi and Prambanan in one day?

Yes, and the three-site tour on this page lists all three, but it is a 12-hour march. The driving between the three sites eats into your temple time. If one site matters most to you, give it its own day.

How do I get to Jomblang Cave from Yogyakarta?

Jomblang is in Semanu, Gunungkidul, about 1.5-2 hours southeast of the city. A listing with a fixed early start is the practical choice, because the light beam shows most reliably between about 10:00 and 12:00 and daily numbers are capped.

Is Setumbu Hill part of the Borobudur ticket?

No. Setumbu Hill is a separate site about 4 km west of Borobudur, with its own ticket of around IDR 60,000 for foreigners. Temple entry is sold separately, so if you want both the hill sunrise and the temple, plan and pay for both.
