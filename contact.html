Site corrections

Send corrections or broken-link reports to rama@yogyakarta-temple-tours.com. Include the page address, what appears wrong and a source for the correction where possible.

Bookings, changes and refunds

This site does not take reservations, payments or cancellation requests. Bookings through our links are handled by Viator. For an existing reservation, use the contact details in your Viator confirmation and provide your booking reference.

Planning your trip

Use the Plan Your Trip guide and the Borobudur, Prambanan and Merapi guides in the menu to compare routes. Start with the sight that anchors your day, then compare what each tour includes, where it starts and how long it runs. For a short trip, the Borobudur against Prambanan comparison explains which temple fits a single free day and when doing both in one day is realistic.

We cannot access your Viator booking details, change a reservation, or promise a reply time for editorial enquiries. Corrections are read and checked, and the page is updated when the listing or the rule behind it has changed. If a price, an inclusion or a start time differs from what this site says, that report is the most useful thing you can send.

What this site is

Yogyakarta Temple Tours is an independent guide to day tours from Yogyakarta, written by Rama Kusuma and funded by affiliate commission on Viator bookings. It does not sell tours, hold inventory or employ guides. Every tour described here is operated and sold by a separate company on Viator.
