By Rama Kusuma. Javanese temple guide based in Sleman, Yogyakarta. This page helps you decide which tour to book for each of your three days in Yogyakarta, and which Day 3 suits the way you like to travel.

The Short Answer: How I Would Split Three Days

If you have three days, give Day 1 to the two big temples, Day 2 to Merapi in the morning and street food at night, and pick Day 3 by mood. For Day 1, consider the Borobudur Temple Climb To The Top & Prambanan day tour, whose listing includes the climb ticket. For Day 2, plan a Merapi jeep and the small-group night food tour. On Day 3, consider the Jomblang and Pindul cave tour for a physical outing, or Menoreh Village Cycling for rice fields, village life and Javanese food. Otherwise, return to a temple you felt you rushed.

The order leaves the temples a dedicated day. Merapi's jeep tracks are dusty, so allow time afterwards to shower and rest before the evening tour. Choose Day 3 according to your energy and interests.

Since 2023, climbing onto Borobudur's terraces needs a separate timed ticket with a capped daily quota, a local guide, and woven upanat sandals. If the climb is the reason you came, confirm it before arranging the rest. My guide to climb-up tours and confirming terrace access explains how to check which listings include the climb.

Day 1, The Temples: Borobudur, Mendut and Prambanan

Borobudur sits roughly 40 km northwest of the city and Prambanan about 17 km east, so visiting both involves driving across the region. This listing names three stops: Borobudur, Mendut, and Prambanan. It says guests get priority access to climb Borobudur, with the entry ticket to the top included. The listed duration is 480 minutes. It gives no pickup details, so confirm pickup and your timed climb slot in writing when booking.

Borobudur Temple Climb To The Top & Prambanan: Who It Suits

Who should pick it: visitors who want both UNESCO temples and a listing that includes the Borobudur climb ticket. About 3 km east of Borobudur, Mendut holds a 3 m seated Buddha flanked by two bodhisattvas. Who should skip it: anyone set on Setumbu Hill sunrise, because this listing does not mention it. Setumbu is a separate site about 4 km west of Borobudur, with its own ticket and a gate that opens around 04:00.

At the top of Setumbu in the dry season of 2017, a retired German couple I was guiding asked me to name the finest temple in Java. Out of habit I started to say Borobudur, then stopped. I told them it depends what you came to feel: Prambanan reaches up like a prayer, and Borobudur asks you to walk inward. The husband said he trusted me more for not selling him a winner. I still think that is the right way to plan Day 1. Do not treat Prambanan as the afterthought at the end of a long day.

At Prambanan, consider walking to Sewu at the north end of the park early. At the main temples, look for the Ramayana reliefs along the inner balustrades of the Shiva and Brahma temples. I would keep Merapi off this day. Combining Borobudur, Merapi and Prambanan is possible, but it becomes a 12-hour outing with less time at each temple.

Day 2, The Volcano Morning and the Food Night

Merapi lava jeeps run on the southern slope around Kaliurang and Kaliadem, over tracks scoured by the 2010 pyroclastic flows. Common stops include the Kaliadem bunker, Museum Sisa Hartaku, and Batu Alien, a large boulder thrown by the eruption. Sunrise departures are around 04:30. Access zones change with alert levels; choose an operator that stays within the permitted radius set by BPPTKG, the volcano monitoring agency.

Small-Group Walking and Food Tour by Night: Who It Suits

Jeeps are priced per vehicle, not per person, and each seats about 4 passengers. A couple pays the same jeep rate as four people, so ask about sharing or budget for the higher cost per head. The black volcanic dust gets everywhere, so leave white shoes at the hotel. Bring cash for the jeep. My Merapi jeep tours comparison explains the route options.

Leave time to shower and rest before the evening outing. The night food listing says you join a guide and no more than seven other travellers in the city centre, then board a becak rickshaw to tour the sights. It describes Javanese food as light and sweet, alongside traditional games and fairground rides at Alun-Alun Kidul. Its listed 180-240 minutes covers the whole experience, including the becak ride, food and activities.

Who should pick it: travellers interested in a guided evening of Javanese street food with a small group. Who should skip it: travellers who would rather cook, or who will be too tired after a pre-dawn jeep for a 3-4-hour evening tour. In that case, eat near your hotel and save your energy for Day 3.

Day 3, Option A: Jomblang and Pindul Caves

Consider this option if you want a physical outing. The listing describes Jomblang as a vertical cave about 65 meters deep, with a beam of light falling through another opening. Afterwards it offers either Pindul Cave body rafting or a return to your hotel. It gives an early morning departure, says the drive takes about 2 hours, and asks for a correct WhatsApp number when booking. The listed duration is 600 minutes.

Jomblang Cave and Pindul Cave Tour from Yogyakarta: Who It Suits

Jomblang is in Semanu, Gunungkidul. Entry involves a rope descent of roughly 60 m using a single-rope technique, with a belay system operated by the local crew. Heaven's Light falls in the connecting Grubug chamber and appears most reliably on sunny days between about 10:00 and 12:00. The visitor cap in that window is commonly cited at around 25. A reservation secures your planned visit; it cannot secure sunshine.

Who should pick it: travellers comfortable with a harness and rope descent who can book ahead and check the weather. Confirm whether you want the optional Pindul body rafting or the hotel return described in the listing. Who should skip it: anyone uneasy about hanging on a rope, unable to spare the full day, or chiefly interested in seeing the light beam during cloudy weather. Day 3, Option B or C: Menoreh Cycling or a Slow Temple Morning

The Menoreh listing describes cycling through a village surrounded by rice fields, rivers, and the Menoreh Hills. It says participants can join local farmers planting or harvesting, meet neighbours, and try tempe benguk, fried tofu, and jamu, a herbal drink made with Indonesian spices. Its highlights include the Nanggulan rice fields and the Mataram river. The listed duration is 180 minutes.

Menoreh Village Cycling: Who It Suits

Who should pick it: travellers interested in rice farming, village life and Javanese food. Confirm departure time, distance, terrain and exertion with the operator before booking. Who should skip it: travellers who prefer not to cycle. If you have not ridden in years, those operator answers will help you judge whether the ride suits you. My cycling tours page compares other routes.

The third option is an independent temple visit. Consider a slower temple morning, planning around opening hours and any timed climb slot. Spend a slower morning at Borobudur on a structure ticket, which covers the courtyard as well as a timed climb to the upper terraces. The courtyard-only ticket is listed for domestic visitors only. The 72 perforated stupas stand on the upper circular terraces. You could also spend longer at Mendut. If hiring a driver, agree on the plan while allowing for those access requirements.

If you are undecided, use your interests and energy as the guide. Consider the caves if you are comfortable with the descent and the forecast is sunny. Cycle if rice farming and village life appeal. If you left a temple feeling you had only skimmed it, go back.

Practical Notes: Tickets, Timing and What to Bring

Tickets. For an independent visit, book the Borobudur structure ticket online before you travel. The official structure ticket page lists climb hours of 08:30 to 17:00 WIB and asks you to arrive 30 to 45 minutes before your session. The recommended tour lists that ticket as included, so confirm your climb slot with the operator before buying a separate ticket. The courtyard-only ticket does not include climbing. Check hours and ticket rules on the official Borobudur Park site. The official Prambanan Free Borobudur package lists both temples' entrance tickets, but its page does not say whether the Borobudur part includes climbing, how long it stays valid or what it saves, so check those terms before choosing it over a structure ticket.

Timing. Prambanan opens around 06:30. On Mondays its Brahma, Shiva and Vishnu main temple areas close for maintenance while the courtyard stays open, according to InJourney's Prambanan guidance, dated 29 July 2026. Regular Borobudur climb sessions start at 08:30 WIB, and dawn on the terraces needs the separate official sunrise ticket, which starts at 04:00 WIB and is limited to 100 people a day. Plan terrace photography around your confirmed slot. Setumbu offers a separate sunrise viewpoint for the temple silhouette and morning mist. If considering the Ramayana Ballet near Prambanan, the show starts around 19:30 and needs a separate ticket. Confirm the open-air or indoor venue before booking. My best time to visit guide explains seasonal planning.

What to bring. Bring water for temple visits and a layer for Merapi's cooler slopes. Wear clothes and shoes you do not mind getting dusty on the jeep. Bring cash for Merapi and confirm payment arrangements for other activities with the operator. For Jomblang, book about a week ahead and check the forecast before departure. Keep its weather-dependent light beam separate from the question of having a confirmed reservation.

  • Sunrise or no sunrise Check whether your Day 1 tour actually goes to Setumbu Hill.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is three days enough for Yogyakarta?

Use this three-day plan for a temple day, a Merapi morning with an optional evening food tour, and a chosen extra. Confirm climb slots and operator timings before booking. Leave the final day flexible if you prefer more temple time.

Can I do Borobudur, Merapi and Prambanan in one day?

It is possible, but I would put Merapi on its own morning so the temples get the time they deserve.

Does Setumbu Hill sunrise include Borobudur entry?

No. They are separate sites, sold separately.

Which Day 3 option is best for families?

Jomblang involves a roughly 60 m rope descent. For cycling, confirm age requirements and route difficulty with the operator before booking for children. Consider a slower temple morning, planning around opening hours and any timed climb slot.

What should I wear on the Merapi jeep tour?

Wear clothes and shoes you do not mind getting dusty, because the 2010 lava tracks are black volcanic dust. Bring a layer for the cooler slopes.
